ABOUT US

Welcome to WanderWheels!

At WanderWheels, we're more than just a car rental service—we're your trusted partner in exploring the vibrant beauty of Cebu. Our mission is to provide seamless, unforgettable travel experiences with modern, reliable vehicles tailored to your adventure. Whether it’s a family getaway, a business trip, or a solo exploration, we’ve got you covered.

Safety First

Your safety is our top priority. We ensure every vehicle undergoes thorough maintenance and inspections to guarantee a safe driving experience.

Customer-Centric

Our friendly team is here for you, providing personalized service and support to make every rental experience exceptional.

Integrity & Transparency

We pride ourselves on clear communication and fair pricing. As a fully registered company, there are no hidden fees—just transparent service.

What We Offer

From compact cars to spacious SUVs, our fleet includes modern models from 2020 to 2024. You can choose self-drive options for complete freedom or select an all-inclusive package with professional drivers who know Cebu inside out. We’re conveniently located in Mandaue, with flexible pickup and delivery services, including Mactan Cebu International Airport, the port, or your hotel.

Join Us on Your Journey

Explore Cebu like never before—from pristine beaches to the bustling city streets. Thank you for choosing WanderWheels—we look forward to helping you create unforgettable memories
